the candid backstory

Small on purpose, honest by design.

Candor Digital didn't start with a pitch deck. It started with a simple rule: never claim a result we can't actually show someone. Every idea gets tested on a real brand first, the numbers get watched closely, and only what genuinely moves them becomes part of how we work — before any of it turns into a service worth paying for.

That's still how this practice operates. Small, provable moves before big claims. A short trial before a long contract. A straight answer before a sales pitch. Not because "candor" sounded like a good brand word — but because it's the only way a new, small practice earns the right to charge for its opinion.

So here's the candid version of our own pitch: we're new, we're small on purpose, and every result on this site from here on will be one we can actually show you — not one we're asking you to take on faith.

Ad spend is never marked up

Your Meta or Google budget is billed straight to your card. Our fee is our fee, and it's the only thing we invoice.

You own every account

Ad accounts, pixels, analytics and content stay in your name from day one. If you leave, nothing leaves with us.

Reports a busy owner can read

One page in plain English plus a five-minute video: what we changed, what it cost, what happened, what's next.

A short list of clients

We cap how many businesses we take on, so the person who runs your audit is the person doing the work.
