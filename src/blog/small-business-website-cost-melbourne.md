"How much for a website?" is a bit like "how much for a car?" The honest answer is that it depends what you need it to do. But you should still get a real number, so here's what the market looks like in 2026.

## The price ranges

| Option | Typical cost | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| DIY builder (Wix, Squarespace, Shopify) | $0–$50/month plus your time | Getting started, very simple needs, tight budgets |
| Template site from a freelancer | about $1,000–$3,000 | A clean, basic site that's live quickly |
| Custom site from a freelancer or small studio | about $2,000–$8,000 | Most small service businesses |
| Agency build | about $4,000–$15,000+ | Bigger sites, complex features, larger teams |

Most Australian small businesses land somewhere between **$2,000 and $8,000** for a properly built five-to-ten-page site. You'll see cheaper offers advertised ($399 or $999 "website in a week"), and they can be fine for a simple start. Just check exactly what's included and who owns the result.

## The costs people forget

The build is a one-off. These aren't:

- **Domain name:** around $15–$40 a year for a .com.au.
- **Hosting:** from free (static sites on good hosting) to $20–$50+ a month for managed WordPress hosting.
- **Updates and maintenance:** plugins, security and small text changes. Some providers charge a monthly retainer; some charge by the hour.
- **Email:** business email such as info@yourbusiness.com.au is usually a separate small monthly cost.

Ask every provider to put the yearly running costs in writing, not just the build price.

## What actually changes the price

1. **Number of pages.** A five-page site and a 25-page site are different jobs.
2. **Who writes the words.** If you hand over finished copy, it's cheaper. Most owners don't, and good copy is half of what makes a site convert.
3. **Features.** Online booking, payments, a shop, member logins and integrations all add time.
4. **Custom design versus a template.** A template is faster and cheaper. Custom design costs more, and it's what makes you look different from the business down the road.

## Questions to ask before you pay anything

- **Do I own the website and the domain?** Some cheap deals keep the site on the provider's account, so if you leave, you lose it.
- **Can I edit the text myself?** You shouldn't need to pay someone to change your opening hours.
- **Is it fast on a phone?** Most of your visitors will be on mobile. Ask to see a past project's Google PageSpeed score.
- **Is the basic SEO set up?** Page titles, descriptions, a sitemap, a Google Business Profile link and analytics should be part of the build, not an add-on.
- **What does it cost to run each year?** Get it in writing.
